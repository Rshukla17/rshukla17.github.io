About

Statistical rigor, engineering execution

I hold a B.S. in Statistics and an M.S. in Information Technology from Missouri State University. That combination shapes how I work: the statistics degree taught me to ask whether a result is real; the IT degree taught me to build the systems that produce it.

Right now I'm a Data Engineering Intern at CoxHealth, building metadata-driven pipelines for an enterprise data migration, while leading research on causal inference in financial and healthcare systems as a Graduate Research Assistant. Previously I shipped an NLP model that routes 11M+ patient messages at 98% accuracy, and ran A/B tests at O'Reilly Auto Parts tied to $10M+ in year-over-year revenue.

Outside of work I train students in Python and R for national analytics competitions — and I've won the USITC Business Analytics Competition three years in a row.
